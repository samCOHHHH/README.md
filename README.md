# 🧠 Introduction

Artificial Intelligence is transforming consulting workflows.

Rather than replacing consultants, AI systems augment and accelerate the traditional consulting process by improving:

- Research
- Analysis
- Client Delivery

The consultant remains responsible for:
- strategic thinking
- judgment
- interpretation
- final recommendations

AI acts as:
- an accelerator
- a co-pilot
- a synthesis engine
- an ideation assistant

---

# ⚡ The AI-Augmented Consulting Model

Traditional consulting revolves around 3 main pillars:

| Pillar | AI Contribution | Main Risks |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Multi-source synthesis | Hallucinations |
| Analysis | Faster insights & scenarios | Oversimplification |
| Client Delivery | Narrative generation | Generic outputs |

---

# 🧩 The GSCE Prompting Framework

The most important framework in the course.

GSCE helps structure prompts professionally.

| Letter | Meaning | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| G | Goal | What the AI should produce |
| S | Style | Tone, structure, format |
| C | Context | Business context & constraints |
| E | Examples | Sample outputs |

---

# Example GSCE Prompt

```text
[CONTEXT]
Our client is a US retailer considering expansion into Europe.

[GOAL]
Generate a competitive landscape analysis including:
- top competitors
- strengths
- weaknesses
- barriers to entry

[STYLE]
MECE structure, analytical tone, comparative table.

[EXAMPLES]
Here is an example of the expected output.
```

---

# 🧪 Why GSCE Matters

Without structure:
- prompts become vague
- outputs become generic
- hallucinations increase

Good prompting dramatically improves:
- accuracy
- structure
- relevance
- business usefulness

# Hallucinations & Verification

---

# What Is a Hallucination?

A hallucination occurs when AI generates information not supported by sources.

Example:
- fake statistics
- invented studies
- nonexistent quotes
- fabricated business insights

---

# How to Reduce Hallucinations

## Always Verify Critical Information

Especially:
- numbers
- dates
- sources
- financial metrics

---

## Ask the AI to Cite Sources

```text
Show me where this information comes from.
```

---

## Challenge the AI

```text
Why could this conclusion be incorrect?
```

---

## Cross-Check Sources

Never trust a single source.

---

# Research Narratives

---

# Purpose of a Narrative

A strong narrative:
- explains what matters

---

# Pyramid Principle

One of the most important consulting frameworks.

---

# Structure

```text
Main Message
 ├── Supporting Argument 1
 ├── Supporting Argument 2
 └── Supporting Argument 3
```

---

# Key Principle

Start with the conclusion first.

Not:
- data first
- explanation first

But:
- insight first

---

# Example

## Top Line

"The company must reduce operational costs by 20% within 2 years."

## Supporting Points

- supply chain inefficiencies
- declining margins
- rising logistics costs

---

# MECE

MECE stands for:

- Mutually Exclusive
- Collectively Exhaustive

---

# Mutually Exclusive

No overlap.

Bad segmentation:
- Tech companies
- Small companies

Overlap exists.

---

# Collectively Exhaustive

Nothing missing.

Good segmentation covers the full scope.

---

# SCR Framework

SCR =
- Situation
- Complication
- Resolution

---

# Example SCR Narrative

## Situation

The client is a leading retailer.

## Complication

Online competitors are rapidly gaining market share.

## Resolution

Accelerate digital transformation and optimize customer acquisition.

---

# 1. Blind Trust

AI sounds convincing even when wrong.

Good:
```text
Analyze this market using Porter's Five Forces.
```

# 🔑 Key Learnings

- AI accelerates consulting workflows dramatically
- Prompt engineering is now a core consulting skill
- Human judgment remains irreplaceable
- Verification is mandatory
- Narratives matter more than raw data
- AI is strongest when used iteratively

---

# 🏁 Conclusion

AI does not replace consultants.

It amplifies:
- speed
- synthesis
- ideation
- analysis

But consultants still provide:
- strategic thinking
- business judgment
- client understanding
- final decision-making

# 📌 Final Takeaway
